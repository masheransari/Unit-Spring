Privacy PolicyTerms and Conditions

Conversions and calculators work offline, without an account. Your calculation inputs, history and saved items are not uploaded by UnitSpring. Android builds with advertising enabled use Google services for ads and consent.

Local information

UnitSpring stores recent conversions and calculations, their inputs and contextual parameters, saved unit pairs and calculator modes, and display preferences on your device. Preferences include decimal precision, thousands separators and theme. This information makes it possible to restore your work and use your preferred settings.

UnitSpring has no developer-operated account or cloud-history service. Core history and saved items are not uploaded as a cloud library. The app does not deliberately attach conversion inputs, calculation history or saved calculations to advertising requests, analytics events or crash-report metadata.

Android backup and device transfer

Android system backup or device transfer may include UnitSpring's local history, saved items and settings when enabled by you and supported by your device. The app's backup rules include its local DataStore directory. Advertising and consent SDK preferences are not included by these rules. Backup storage and retention are controlled by Android, your device provider and your backup settings. A backup can restore app data after reinstalling or changing devices.

Advertising-enabled builds

Advertising-enabled Android versions use Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Versions that exclude these advertising SDKs make no AdMob or UMP requests. Other included services, described below, may still use a network connection. Core converters and calculators remain available without advertising.

In advertising-enabled Android builds, UMP contacts Google to determine which privacy notices or consent forms apply. UnitSpring requests updated consent information at launch and presents any required form before requesting ads. If that process fails, UnitSpring pauses advertising for that session. Conversions and calculators continue to work.

When ads are permitted, Google's SDK may collect and share IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and other device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The app does not request precise device location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. Ad and consent services require an internet connection.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures for information about Google's handling of these data. Your choices and regional requirements affect how ads are served; declining personalization does not necessarily eliminate all advertising data processing.

Firebase usage measurement and crash reporting

The Android application includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. These services are separate from the local calculation history. When Analytics collection is enabled, Google may process app usage and interaction information, app or device identifiers, device characteristics and network-derived information to measure how the app is used.

When Crashlytics is enabled, it may process crash stack traces, app and operating-system versions, device characteristics, diagnostic information and installation identifiers to help diagnose reliability issues. Analytics and crash reports can be available to the app developer through Firebase. The app does not deliberately add your conversion history, saved calculations or numerical inputs as custom analytics events or crash-report fields.

Collection depends on the application's Firebase configuration and any applicable consent settings. Advertising privacy options are not a general-purpose Analytics or Crashlytics opt-out. For questions about Firebase-held data, applicable privacy rights, retention or deletion, contact the developer through the support details accompanying the app or hosting website. See Firebase privacy information and Google's Privacy Policy. No Firebase account is required to use the calculators.

Choices and deletion

  • Use Settings → Clear history to remove Recent entries. Saved items are managed and removed separately from the Saved screen.
  • Use Android app storage controls or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Previously created system backups may remain and may restore data; manage backups in your device or backup account settings.
  • Where Google requires it, Settings → Ad privacy options lets you revisit advertising privacy choices. While the form is open, new ad requests are paused and previously loaded interstitials are discarded.
  • Android advertising settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Google and your device provider control their own settings, retention and deletion processes.

Security and retention

Local data is stored in the app's private storage and retained until you remove it or clear the app's data. Recent history is limited to 50 entries. No storage system is guaranteed to be loss-free. Google states that Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit. Its retention and processing are governed by its own policies.

Children

UnitSpring is a general-purpose utility and is not designed or marketed specifically for children. If you believe information about a child has been processed inappropriately, contact the developer using the support details accompanying the app or the website hosting this policy.
